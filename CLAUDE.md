## Current state

Phases 0 (foundation and risk spikes), 1 (CI/CD), 2 (app skeleton and design system), 3 (job queue and upload), 4 (file transcription, in six stages 4.1–4.6 listed in `ROADMAP.md`) and 5 ("Guardar áudio", in stages 5.1 encoder and 5.2 app flow) and 6 (live recording, in stages 6.1 capture and `Recorder` (`src/capture/`), 6.2 recording job and labeled transcript, 6.3 UI and routes (`src/web/record.rs`) and 6.4 the hardware "Pronto quando" with the release exe) are done. Phase 7 (robustness, packaging and final delivery) is in progress, in stages 7.1 logs and panic hook, 7.2 tray app, 7.3 history, `config.json` v1 and cleanup, 7.4 full release (Vulkan delay-load, `--self-test`, `cargo xtask dist`) and 7.5 Windows Sandbox and delivery (listed in `ROADMAP.md`); 7.1 to 7.4 are done, and 7.5 is next. The release is a window-subsystem tray app (no console); the debug build stays a console app. The app transcribes uploads on the dedicated GPU (large-v3-turbo) or the CPU (`small`), in ~30-min blocks so 2–3 h meetings stay under ~1.5 GB of RAM (measured: 1 h in 2 min 9 s on the RTX 3050 and 8 min on the CPU), and the full release (`cargo xtask dist`, also what `release.yml` runs; ~806 MB) has Vulkan, the large-v3-turbo and the `small` embedded, with `vulkan-1.dll` delay-loaded so it opens and transcribes on the CPU where there is no GPU driver (tags only with the user's ok; the published v0.2.0 was CPU-only). The product flow is automatic: receive or record → extract audio → transcribe to `.txt` and `.md` → ask "Guardar o áudio?" on the done card (discard the upload kept in `uploads/`, or encode an AAC `.m4a` saved where the user picks in the Windows "Save as" dialog). Media is disposable: video compression was dropped, and the upload waits for the answer only until the job is removed, the app closes or 24 h pass (`Audio::Expired`, "O áudio foi descartado depois de 24 h sem resposta."). At startup the list is rebuilt from the `job.json` of the 50 most recent output folders (the history). The repo is `filipimr/scriba` (public) with `main` protected (checks `checks` and `deny` required); `README.md` (pt-BR) presents it on GitHub. The `scriba` binary serves the UI:

- `src/transcript/`: segments → paragraphs (a pause > 1.5 s or a speaker change breaks), `render_txt` (UTF-8, CRLF) and its inverse `parse` for the reader, `render_md` (escaped Markdown). A recording (`Header.recording`) has `Gravação: Você (microfone) e Outros (som do PC)` instead of `Arquivo:` and `[hh:mm:ss] Você: …` paragraphs (`**[hh:mm:ss] Você:**` in the `.md`); `parse` reads the `Speaker` only under that header, so an upload never gets a label. `merge.rs` merges the two tracks by time and drops the echo: a "Você" segment is echo when an "Outros" segment crosses its ±1 s window and ≥ 60% of its words (lowercase, no punctuation or accents) appear in order (LCS) in that window's "Outros" text; a long "Você" (≥ 8 words) is also echo when ≥ 60% of it is in one "Outros" segment from the last 10 min, or in the "Você" text of the 30 s before its end (the whisper loop on raw echo); "Outros" never changes. `output.rs` holds the folder name `AAAA-MM-DD HHmm - nome` (`- Gravação` for a recording; `clean_name` for Windows, " (2)" on collision), atomic writes, and the `job.json` manifest v1 (`media`: `audio`, `video` or `recording`); `write` removes the folder if anything fails.
- `src/asr/`: transcription. `backend.rs` reads the ggml device registry (`devices()`, via whisper-rs `raw-api`) and the pure `plans()` (dedicated GPUs by memory → CPU with `small`; the integrated GPU is left out, slower than the CPU in the 4.3 benchmark; a candidate needs its model); a GPU candidate is checked through the whisper.cpp log (`log.rs`), since a failed GPU silently falls back to CPU. `vad.rs` runs Silero itself (whisper-rs `state.full` ignores `params.vad`), loaded from memory, splices the speech and maps times back (`TimeMap::offset` adds the block start). Long audio goes in blocks of ~30 min (`Asr::blocks` changes the size; tests use seconds): the `Samples` trait reads each window (from a slice or the `Spool`), the VAD runs on it and `blocks.rs` cuts in the middle of the longest pause in its last sixth (else the last pause in the window, else a hard cut), so RAM follows the block, not the duration, and paragraphs at the seam break as in the original. `cache.rs` is `IdleCache` (unload after 10 min idle), `phantom.rs` the hallucination filter, `models.rs` embedded or `models/` models. Progress and abort use our own C trampolines (whisper-rs 0.16's "safe" ones leak, and the abort one is type-confused).
- `src/capture/` (Phase 6): live recording in two WAV tracks, `mic.wav` ("Você") and `sistema.wav` ("Outros", the output's WASAPI loopback, with silence playing on that output so the loopback never stops delivering), 32 kHz mono 16-bit, in `<data-dir>/gravacoes/<id>/` with `gravacao.json` (`storage.rs`; state `gravando`, `parada` or `transcrita` (`mark_transcribed`); `recover` finds recordings left by a crash or Ctrl+C, deletes empty and already transcribed ones and leftover `.f32`). `source.rs` holds the `AudioSource` trait (`devices`, `now_ns` on the packets' clock, `open` per track with an `Events` sender carrying a generation number) and the SPSC rings (`rtrb`, 10 s) the callbacks only copy into. `wasapi.rs` is `CpalSource` (cpal 0.18: f32/i16/i32 streams, capture timestamp = QPC of the first frame, Bluetooth from cpal's `InterfaceType` or the endpoint's `DEVPKEY_Device_EnumeratorName` starting with `BTH`). `align.rs` is pure: downmix, i16, RMS level, and the `Aligner` that pins each track to the clock (`(t − t0) × 32000`): a gap over 8 ms becomes silence at once, drift between 2 and 8 ms is corrected over 5 s by `swr_set_compensation` (`media::Resampler`, FFI on swr), an early packet loses its start. `writer.rs` is the `scriba-gravacao` thread that owns the streams: drains the rings every 20 ms, resamples to 32 kHz, writes with `hound` (`flush` every 5 s), publishes levels and "mic silent for 10 s", reopens a track on `Invalidated` (default device changed), ends on `Lost`, disk below 512 MB or the 12 h cap, and pads both tracks to the same end (not after a full disk). It keeps Windows from idle-sleeping while recording; after a suspension anyway, a gap over 30 s takes the suspended time out of both tracks (`Aligner::skip`, the recording goes on, `Status::Recording.suspended`), and a timestamp over 1 s in the future re-anchors only its own track; `TrackStats` (startup latency, filled gaps, max error, drift span, lost packets) go into the `Recording`. `mod.rs` is the `Recorder` (one recording at a time, `start`/`stop`/`status`/`finish_on_exit`, `on_finish` gets every finished recording but the Ctrl+C one). `fake.rs` (`cfg(test)` or `test-support`) is `FakeSource`: scripted signal (tones, impulses at the same instants on both tracks, given samples), rates and channels, delivery and timestamp jitter, gaps, ppm drift, an `Invalidated`/`Lost` at a given time, and a clock that can run faster than real time; `Signal::Pulsing` is a tone whose volume rises and falls (the meters move). `ScriptedSource` (same cfg) is the `--fake-audio` source: `Script::app()` in real time (two pulsing tones, the simulated devices, one of them Bluetooth) plus the words in `<data-dir>/audio-falso.txt`, re-read on each open: `perder-mic`/`perder-saida` (the device is lost 2 s after opening), `sem-microfone`, `microfone-bloqueado`, `mic-mudo`.
- `src/clock.rs`: local time via `GetLocalTime`/`GetSystemTime`, with the UTC offset from their difference; `LocalTime::parse_rfc3339` (the inverse of `rfc3339`) and `unix_seconds`; the `Clock` trait (`SystemClock`, and `FakeClock` with `advance` under `cfg(test)`/`test-support`).
- `src/cleanup.rs` (Phase 7.3): at startup, deletes the `--self-test` temp files in `%TEMP%` and the `*.m4a.parte` in the history folders and `audio_dir` older than 1 day.
- `src/logs.rs` (Phase 7.1): `<data-dir>\logs\scriba.log` (`Paths::logs_dir`), rotated by `RotatingFile` above 5 MB keeping 4 old files (`scriba.1.log` is the newest). The capture is at the handle level: stdout and stderr go through two pipes (`SetStdHandle`, and `_open_osfhandle` + `_dup2` on the static CRT's fd 1 and 2, so whisper.cpp, ggml and FFmpeg land there too, with no `eprintln!` changed); one thread per pipe writes each line with `AAAA-MM-DD hh:mm:ss.mmm` in front and repeats it raw on the previous destination (a duplicate of the original handle: the console, in UTF-16, or the caller's pipe, so the E2E and `tests/upload.rs` still read `Scriba em …`). The panic hook (thread, `Location`, message, backtrace) and the `SetUnhandledExceptionFilter` write straight to the file. Dropping `Logs` restores the handles and drains the pipes.

## Environment and commands

- One-time setup, in an admin PowerShell: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\setup.ps1`. It is idempotent; `-LimparVcpkg` deletes vcpkg temp files; `-Sandbox` only enables the Windows Sandbox (`Containers-DisposableClientVM`) and exits, warning when a restart is needed. It installs the toolchain and sets the user variables `VCPKG_ROOT=C:\dev\vcpkg`, `LIBCLANG_PATH`, and `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=C:\dev\t\scriba`. The short target dir is required: inside the repo, the whisper.cpp Vulkan build exceeds MAX_PATH.
- Static FFmpeg (once, then cached): `C:\dev\vcpkg\vcpkg.exe install --triplet x64-windows-static-release --x-install-root=vcpkg_installed`. The triplet is the release-only overlay in `triplets/` (declared in `vcpkg.json`); debug libs were never linked. In manifest mode, installing another triplet replaces `vcpkg_installed/`, so an environment from before Phase 3.5 reinstalls FFmpeg once with this command.
- `.cargo/config.toml` holds the build env: triplet `x64-windows-static-release`, `+crt-static`, `FFMPEG_DIR`, the whisper.cpp `GGML_*` SIMD baseline, `CMAKE_MSVC_RUNTIME_LIBRARY`, and `CMAKE_{C,CXX}_FLAGS_RELEASE`. Without those last two, the release build of whisper.cpp comes out unoptimized (`/Od`): cmake-rs strips `/O*` and overwrites the Release flags under the Visual Studio generator. Changing `GGML_*`/`CMAKE_*` needs `cargo clean -p whisper-rs-sys` (plus `--release` for the release profile), because the build script does not track them; `release.yml` does this clean before building.
- `cargo xtask model` downloads the models to `models/` and checks SHA-256; `--only` takes comma-separated name fragments (`--only base` is what CI uses; `--only small` fetches the CPU-model candidate). `cargo xtask fixtures` regenerates `tests/fixtures/`; `--only` takes comma-separated file names (e.g. `--only fala.mkv,pausas.wav`) and reuses the existing `fala.wav`, so new fixtures never change the current ones. The recording tracks (32 kHz mono 16-bit, Maria's phrases alternating "Outros"/"Você"; `voce-com-eco.wav` adds "Outros" at −12 dB, 60 ms later) come from `--only voce.wav,outros.wav,voce-com-eco.wav` (the echo reads the other two). It needs the "Microsoft Maria" SAPI voice and the `ffmpeg` CLI.
- Lint: `cargo fmt --all --check` and `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --features "spikes/vulkan scriba/test-support" -- -D warnings`.
- Tests: `cargo nextest run --workspace --features "spikes/vulkan scriba/test-support"` (`tests/upload.rs` has `required-features = ["test-support"]`). ## How work proceeds

- One phase at a time. Start a phase only when the user asks, with its own detailed plan approved before execution, and do not roll into the next phase when it finishes.
- When a phase starts, set its row in the `ROADMAP.md` status table to "Em andamento". When it finishes, tick its checklists, set it to "Concluída", and record decisions and measured numbers in "Registro de decisões".
- A feature is done only when it has automated tests at the right level (see "Qualidade e testes" in `ROADMAP.md`) and CI is green. Tests that need real audio devices or a GPU belong in the local hardware suite, not in CI.
- If a spike or test invalidates a decision, update `ROADMAP.md` before continuing.

## Design system (`DESIGN-scriba.md`)

`DESIGN-scriba.md` is a DESIGN.md-style document in two parts:

- **YAML front matter** holds the design tokens, with English keys: `colors`, `typography`, `rounded`, `spacing`, `motion`, `elevation`, and `components`. Components reference tokens with `{group.token}` syntax, e.g. `backgroundColor: "{colors.primary}"`, and variants are separate keys (`button-primary-active`, `record-button-recording`). Phase 2 turns these tokens into CSS via `cargo xtask css`.
- **Markdown body** (pt-BR) explains the system: brand and logo, iconography, colors, typography, layout, elevation, shapes, motion, components, accessibility, microcopy, do's and don'ts, iteration guide, and known gaps.

It is the Scriba app's own design system, inspired by Apple's visual language: one blue accent for actions, pills, Inter with negative tracking, and a monochrome brand. The source logo is `docs/brand/logo-original.jpg`.

## Editing `DESIGN-scriba.md`

- Keep the front matter and the prose in sync. A token or component changed in YAML must be reflected in the body sections that describe it, and vice versa.
- Reference tokens with `{...}` rather than raw hex values in components. The body repeats hex values for readability; keep those matching the YAML.
- Follow the rules the spec sets:
  - `colors.primary` is the only action color; status colors mark state, never actions (sole exception: `button-danger` inside `confirm-dialog`).
  - Weight ladder 400/600.
  - Body text at 17px.
  - No gradients; shadows only on `toast` and `confirm-dialog`.
  - All text meets WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
  - Light and dark themes: `colors:` is the light theme, `themes.dark` overrides only differing colors. Every new color needs a dark value. Blue text uses `colors.link`; `colors.primary` is only for fills.
- Document default, pressed, focus, disabled, and error states, not hover.
- Preserve the "Lacunas conhecidas" section and update it when a gap is filled rather than deleting it silently.
